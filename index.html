About the Town

The story behind Ciudad de Paseo

Our Story

✦

Founded in 2024, Ciudad de Paseo was born from a dream shared by a group of Filipino gamers: to build a western world that felt alive, authentic, and truly ours. The name carries our identity — "Bayan ng Alamat at Kasaysayan," the Town of Legend and History.

Set in the twilight of the American frontier, our town breathes with horse-drawn carriages, flickering gas lamps, cathedral bells, and the distant sound of a piano drifting from the saloon. Here, players write their own stories — some noble, some notorious, all unforgettable.

We are a serious roleplay community that values immersion, respect, and creativity above all. No matter your role — from the sheriff keeping the peace to the outlaw riding at dawn — you belong in Paseo.

The Law of the Land

Mga Batas ng Ciudad de Paseo — read carefully before you ride

These rules exist to protect the story we build together. Ignorance of the law is no excuse — by joining Ciudad de Paseo, you agree to uphold every rule below. Breaking them may result in warnings, kicks, or a permanent ban from town. Ride honorably.

Law Enforcement Participation

  • Prior to the engagement, Law Enforcement must have a sufficient number of participants. Once the gunfight has started, no additional participants may join or be added to either side.
  • +1 Rule: In every encounter, Law Enforcement may have a maximum of one (1) additional participant. The +1 participant can participate in the firefight after the hostage has been safely released.

No Cross-RP

  • Cross-RP from Non-Law Enforcement is strictly prohibited during active robberies and hold-up situations. Any player caught Cross-RPing during these situations will be penalized.

Hostage Limit

  • Only one (1) hostage is allowed per encounter.

Weapons & Hostages

  • Guardia Civil must keep their weapons holstered until the hostage has been safely released.

Hostage Safety

  • Killing a hostage during a robbery is strictly prohibited. Hostages must be surrendered to Law Enforcement once the robbery or encounter has been resolved — unless the hostage is not complying and is also part of the roleplay. No finishing off or blowing their heads off.

Escape & Pursuit

  • If an attempt to escape occurs during the robbery, only the Law Enforcement officers who initially responded to the encounter may pursue the criminals. No additional Law Enforcement may join the pursuit.
  • Exception: If bandits leave the town where they committed a robbery, law enforcement must pursue them with full force to ensure they understand the consequences of their actions and to deter future crimes.

Event Timer

  • If no Law Enforcement arrives after 15 minutes (including the trigger point), the robbers are free to leave and escape the robbery scene.

Medical & New Life Event

  • If a player's head has been blown off accidentally, Doctors cannot revive them. This will result in a New Life Event (NLE).
  • If the player's head remains intact, Doctors may revive them only at the Police Station for processing.

Police Station Escape

  • Any attempt to escape from the Police Station after being revived for processing will be considered Fail RP — unless there was a story created through realistic roleplay.

No Headshots on Fallen Players

  • Guardia Civil are strictly prohibited from shooting the head of fallen or incapacitated enemies. Fallen suspects must be properly processed and taken to jail after receiving treatment from a Doctor.

Weapons & Evidence

  • Guardia Civil may confiscate at least one (1) weapon from each robber. Any additional weapons may be roleplayed as being confiscated and removed from the scene to prevent further harmful actions.
  • Other items that can reasonably be used as evidence may also be looted or confiscated by Guardia Civil, provided the action is properly roleplayed and has a valid RP reason.

Compliance & Robbery

  • Realistic roleplay is required before engaging in a civilian robbery. Low-quality or unrealistic roleplay may be considered Fail RP or Force RP.
  • Bandits cannot kill civilians or other players who fully comply with their demands.
  • During a robbery, bandits may only loot one (1) item from each victim. (No looting of Regalo ni Manoy / Ayuda.)
  • Bandits may only take money, food, items obtainable from grinds, and weapons that are visibly seen/equipped by the victim. (1 item only, as mentioned above.)
  • Donated weapons are exempt from being looted.
  • If a weapon is visibly equipped and capable of causing harm, you may roleplay removing/securing that weapon.
  • Unequipped weapons must be left with the player.
  • If an unequipped weapon goes missing during or after an encounter, the affected player should submit a ticket for staff assistance.

Non-Compliance

  • If a victim refuses to comply, you are permitted to kill them.
  • However, if the victim is killed because of their refusal to comply, bandits may only loot one (1) item from each victim. (No looting of Regalo ni Manoy / Ayuda and donated weapons.)
  • During a holdup or robbery, shooting or blowing off a victim's head is strictly prohibited.

Finishing Off Downed Players

  • Once a player is dead or incapacitated, no one may intentionally shoot them for the purpose of blowing off their head or "finishing them off."
  • Accidental head loss during an active firefight is permitted if it occurs naturally as part of the combat.
  • Intentionally targeting a dead or incapacitated player's head is considered Fail RP.

Roleplay After Death & Revival

  • After being revived, the player is considered incapacitated and recovering from their injuries.
  • A revived player cannot immediately return to combat or begin shooting.
  • Bandits or other players may not immediately attack a player who has just been revived — doing so is considered Fail RP.
  • If a player was killed specifically due to head injuries, they should have no memory of the incident after respawning/revival.
  • The player may only regain knowledge of the incident if another player who witnessed the event roleplays telling them what happened.

Accidental Beheading

  • If a player's head is accidentally lost during a firefight, Doctors must retrieve the visually beheaded body and roleplay transporting it to the cemetery for burial.
  • Accidental beheading must not be intentionally caused or exploited for additional RP advantages.

Combat Logging

  • Combat logging is strictly prohibited until the area is clear of all other players involved in the encounter.
  • Players must remain in the area and continue roleplay until the situation has naturally concluded.

👮 Required Law Enforcement

  • +1 based on the robbers' count. Guardia Civil must be online and available before a bank robbery can be initiated.
  • The Guardia Civil must respond to the robbery and may attempt to negotiate, surround the bank, or apprehend the suspects.
  • Guardia Civil must conduct the response through proper roleplay and may not use out-of-character information.

🔫 Robbery & Combat Rules

  • A minimum of 8, maximum of 12 robbers only.
  • The robbery must be initiated through proper roleplay before any shots are fired.
  • Robbers must give Guardia Civil a reasonable opportunity to respond or negotiate.
  • Random shooting without proper roleplay initiation is prohibited.
  • Players must value their lives and should not recklessly engage where surrender or retreat would be more realistic.
  • Shooting, hostage-taking, and other escalations must have a valid roleplay reason.
  • Once the robbery has escalated into combat, all parties must continue to follow proper roleplay standards.

🤝 Hostage & Negotiation

  • One hostage required.
  • Hostages must be actual players and may not be fabricated or artificially created.
  • Hostages may not be intentionally harmed without a legitimate roleplay reason.
  • Guardia Civil should attempt reasonable negotiations when hostages are involved.
  • Robbers must make reasonable demands that can be roleplayed.
  • Both sides must allow negotiations to develop naturally rather than immediately forcing a gunfight.

🚔 Getaway & Pursuit

  • Robbers may attempt to escape through proper roleplay.
  • Guardia Civil may pursue suspects using appropriate roleplay.
  • Robbers may not use unrealistic methods, exploits, or glitches to escape.
  • Exception: If bandits leave the town where they committed a robbery, law enforcement must pursue them with full force to deter future crimes.

🥷 Robbery Conclusion

  • Once the robbery has concluded, participants must follow normal server rules and may not immediately initiate unrelated revenge or retaliation based solely on the outcome of the robbery.

  • The Guardia Civil must respond to the robbery and may attempt to negotiate, surround the ship, or apprehend the suspects.

🔫 Robbery & Combat Rules
